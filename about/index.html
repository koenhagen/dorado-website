About

A reason to get out the door

Dorado is for people who find running boring. It turns your usual route into an audio adventure, so a run becomes something you want to do rather than something you make yourself do.

Why we made it

Most running apps are built for people chasing a faster time. We built Dorado for everyone else: the runner who has started before, stalled, and needs a reason to go out at all. We don't measure success in personal bests. If you run more often, run a little longer and keep coming back, Dorado is doing its job.

How it works

Dorado is a world you hear, not a story read to you. Its characters and events unfold around you in spatial audio, and your pace and location decide what happens next. The world reacts to your run, and each time you head out you pick up where you left off.

What you need

A phone and a pair of headphones. No watch and no special gear. Headphones matter, because the sound is placed around you.

Dorado works alongside the apps you already use. Keep your music playing on Spotify and record the run with Strava while Dorado runs on top.
