Get in touch

Questions, feedback from a run, or something that isn’t working: email us at info@dorado.run. A person reads every message.

Questions and feedback

Write to info@dorado.run about anything to do with Dorado: the app, the beta, or this website. If something went wrong during a run, tell us what phone you use and roughly when it happened, so we can find the problem faster.

Want to try Dorado before launch? Join the beta.

Your personal data

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to withdraw consent you gave us. Pick the request below. It opens an email with the subject filled in.

  • Request a copy of my data
  • Correct my data
  • Delete my data
  • Withdraw my consent

Send the request from the email address you use for Dorado, so we can confirm it’s you. If we can’t, we may ask for a little more information before we act on it. We answer privacy requests within one month.

The privacy statement explains what we collect, why, and the rights you have.
