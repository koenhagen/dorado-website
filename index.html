How it works

Extensive story

A reason to run again

A persistent, ongoing story that you return to run after run. Pick up exactly where you left off.

Spatial soundscape

Escape your own street

Sound is placed around you. Our 3D spatial audio puts you right in the jungle. Hear a birdcall above your left shoulder, a river that gets louder ahead.

Reactive narrative

You decide where it goes

Dictate where it goes by how you run. Run towards the mountains, or delve into a canyon. The world responds to your choices.

What you need

What you need

  • An iPhone or an Android phone
  • Earphones, any pair with two ears to them
  • A road you are thoroughly sick of

What you don't need

  • A smartwatch, a Garmin, a chest strap
  • A training plan with weeks in it

It runs alongside, not instead

Dorado is not a music player and not a run tracker, so it does not ask you to give either of them up. Keep Spotify playing over the top. Keep Strava recording underneath. Dorado is the part in between, and all three run together on one phone.
